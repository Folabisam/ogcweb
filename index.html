About the Ogun Conference

The Ogun Conference of the Seventh-day Adventist Church is a member entity of the Western Nigeria Union Conference (WNUC), West-Central Africa Division. Covering Ogun State, we are committed to proclaiming the Three Angels' Messages of Revelation 14:6–12, planting churches, operating schools, and nurturing believers to live holy, mission-driven lives in preparation for the second coming of Jesus Christ.
